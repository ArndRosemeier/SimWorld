# Rooms sound like rooms

- Inside a building you now hear the room. A packed pub roars with voices, cutlery and clinking glasses. A café is softer, a shop hums, and a home is near silent with a few small sounds while someone is awake. A church has a hush and a long echo.
- The murmur grows with the number of people awake on your floor, so an empty bar is quiet and a full one is loud.
- The street, the bells and the weather are now muffled indoors. Before, they played at full volume through the walls.
