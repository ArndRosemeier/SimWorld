# The town has a real calendar and weather

- The game date follows the real calendar: seasons, daylight saving, Easter, Halloween and Christmas fall on their real days, and sunrise and sunset move through the year.
- The town has weather of its own, the same for everyone in it: sunny spells, cloud, drizzle, rain, thunderstorms on summer afternoons, morning fog in autumn, and snow in winter that stays on the ground until it thaws.
- Not on screen yet: the sky and the people start reacting to it in the next changes.
