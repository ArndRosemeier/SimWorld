# Inside the town hall and the farms

- The town hall has a way in now: a wide front door opens on the hall with the public counters, the council chamber (the bench for the mayor and the clerks, the councillors' desks facing it), offices, a meeting room and toilets, with more offices upstairs. Whoever works or has business there is inside when you take them over.
- Barns and sheds have insides: stalls with straw and troughs, stacked hay, the tractor by the door, workbenches, a feed store and the farm office. The farm hands work in the barn instead of standing about the yard.
- Each farmhouse is one family's home over both floors, and you can walk into every farm building.
