# Main roads on the town's edge drawn across their whole width; things stand on the ground

- The main roads round the edge of the town were drawn only up to their middle: the outer half was grass with lane paint floating over it. They now have both carriageway halves and a sidewalk on the outer side, so cars there drive on the road.
- Street lamps, benches, café terraces, manholes, signs, town trees and holiday decorations stand on the ground drawn under them (road, sidewalk, garden or grass), instead of floating or sinking a little.
