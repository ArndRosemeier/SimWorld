# Cars stand on the road

- Cars, buses, bikes, police cars, ambulances and parked cars no longer float above the road or sink into it: every wheel stands on the road surface as it is drawn, on hills, across cambered country roads, on bridges and in driveways.
- Vehicles now tip with the road: they lean uphill and downhill, and tilt sideways on a slope.
- Train carriages tilt with the line on a gradient.
- Cars in car parks stand on the paving instead of sinking into it.
- The barrier posts at level crossings stand on the ground beside the road.
