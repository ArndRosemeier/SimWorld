# Nobody starts under the world

- Taking over someone who is on the road to the city or the next town, or at work there, now puts you at the edge of town where their road leaves it, instead of kilometres out where there is no ground.
- The body always lands on the ground or floor with room around it: never inside a parked car, a post or a closed building.
- Bridges are solid decks with parapets you can walk across, instead of slopes down to the water.
- If the body ever drops below the ground, it is put back where it last stood.
