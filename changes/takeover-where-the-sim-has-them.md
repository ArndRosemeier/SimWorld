# You take over people where they really are

- People who are out of town (at work in the city, at school in the next town) or in a car, on a bus or on a bike are no longer offered in the picker, so nobody starts out in the fields. The count says how many are away right now.
- Taking over a pupil in class, someone at church, in the library or another public building now stands you up inside it, by where they were. Those buildings were missing their walls before, so you landed outside.
- Someone who has just arrived somewhere is taken over inside, not out on the street.
- The fields are walkable as far as the world is drawn: the invisible wall a few hundred metres out of town is gone.
